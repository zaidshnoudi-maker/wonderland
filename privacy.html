Privacy Policy

Liyana's Wonderland · Last updated 3 October 2026

In short: this app does not collect, share or sell any information. It has no ads, no tracking, no accounts and no in-app purchases. Everything a family creates in the app stays on their own device.

Who the app is for

Liyana's Wonderland is a play and learning app for young children (about 2 to 5 years old), used with a parent or carer nearby.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, store on our servers, or share any personal information about children or adults. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no third-party tracking or data-collection tools, and no sign-in.

What is saved on the device only

So that the app can remember play between sessions, it saves the following in the device's own app storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • the child's first name, birthday date (for the party countdown), avatar and pet choices;
  • game progress, stickers and the potty star chart;
  • drawings made in the Art Studio;
  • family voice recordings, if a grown-up chooses to make them (see below).

A grown-up can delete this at any time in the app's grown-up settings ("Erase pictures", deleting a recording, or "Start over"), or by deleting the app.

Microphone

The microphone is used only when a grown-up opens the grown-up settings (behind a grown-up check) and chooses to record "family voice" phrases. The recordings are kept only on the device and are played back inside the app. They are never uploaded or shared.

Photos

The app never reads the photo library. If a grown-up taps "Save pictures to Photos", the device's own share sheet is used to save the child's drawings; nothing is sent to us.

Internet use

The app works offline. The app itself does not send any information over the internet. The internet is only used to download the app and its updates (and, in the web version, when a grown-up taps "Get the latest version"). Those downloads are handled by the app store or the website host, which may keep standard technical logs under their own privacy policies.

Children's privacy

Because we do not collect any personal information, we do not collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU/UK). This is in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR. Settings, links to this policy and the microphone are behind a grown-up check that a young child cannot easily pass.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.
